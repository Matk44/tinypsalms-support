Tiny Psalms

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Tiny Psalms is a bedtime prayer and Bible-inspired story app for families. This Privacy Policy explains what information Tiny Psalms ("Tiny Psalms", "we", "us", or "our") collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and how you can request access, correction, or deletion of your information.

Short version: Tiny Psalms uses account information, a child nickname and age range, selected bedtime themes, optional parent notes, generated stories, generated audio, subscription status, and local reminder settings to provide the app. We do not sell personal information, do not use third-party advertising, do not use cross-app tracking, and do not collect photos, voice recordings, precise location, contacts, camera data, or microphone data.

1. Scope

This Policy applies to the Tiny Psalms mobile app for iOS and Android and the related backend services that support sign-in, story generation, subscriptions, generated audio, saved stories, support, and account deletion requests.

Tiny Psalms is intended to be used by parents and guardians with their children. Parents and guardians are responsible for creating accounts, entering child profile information, and deciding what optional context to provide.

3. AI Story and Audio Processing

Tiny Psalms uses AI service providers to provide core app features. When you generate a story, we may send the child nickname, age range or age number, selected bedtime need, optional parent context, and related prompt instructions to OpenAI through our secure Firebase Cloud Functions backend. We use this information only to create the requested Bible-inspired bedtime story and prayer.

When audio narration is generated, we may send the generated story and prayer text to xAI's text-to-speech service to create an MP3 narration file. The resulting audio file is stored in Firebase Storage under your account so it can be played in the app.

Please do not enter highly sensitive information in optional parent notes. Tiny Psalms is not a medical, mental-health, legal, or emergency service, and generated content is not professional advice.

4. How We Use Information

  • Provide sign-in, account security, and account recovery.
  • Create and personalize age-appropriate bedtime prayers and stories.
  • Save generated stories, generated audio, favorites, and library items.
  • Manage subscriptions, purchases, entitlements, and restore purchases.
  • Schedule local bedtime reminders when you enable them.
  • Respond to support, access, correction, and deletion requests.
  • Maintain app security, prevent abuse or fraud, debug errors, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your personal information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. These include HTTPS connections, Firebase Authentication, Firebase Security Rules, server-side API keys and secrets, access-limited backend services, and service providers that offer encryption in transit and at rest.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe your account or information has been compromised, contact us at dreamprayer.dev@gmail.com.

7. Retention and Deletion

We retain account information, child profile information, generated stories, generated audio, subscription status, and related app data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide Tiny Psalms. Local reminder preferences remain on your device until you change them, clear app data, or uninstall the app.

If you request account deletion, we will delete or de-identify your Tiny Psalms account data, including your Firebase account, child profile, story library, generated story text, generated audio files, favorites, and app-specific subscription association, within 30 days unless we must retain limited information for legal, tax, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or regulatory reasons.

Third-party providers may keep limited records under their own legal, security, abuse-prevention, or transaction-retention obligations. For example, Apple, Google, and RevenueCat may retain purchase records, and AI providers may retain short-term safety or abuse-monitoring logs under their policies.

To request deletion, visit our Account and Data Deletion page or email dreamprayer.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Tiny Psalms account deletion".

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can update child nickname and age range in the app where profile editing is available.
  • You can choose whether to provide optional parent context for a generated story.
  • You can enable, disable, or adjust local bedtime reminders in the app and through device notification settings.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings.
  • You can request access, correction, export, or deletion by emailing us.

Depending on where you live, you may have privacy rights such as the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal information. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children's Privacy

Tiny Psalms is a family app intended for parents and guardians to use with children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without parent or guardian involvement. The app is designed to avoid child photos, voice recordings, precise location, contacts, camera data, and microphone data.

Parents and guardians should use a nickname rather than a full legal name if they prefer. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate parent or guardian consent, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.
