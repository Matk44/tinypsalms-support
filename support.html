What to Include

To help us investigate quickly, please include:

  • The email address connected to your Tiny Psalms account.
  • Your device type, such as iPhone, iPad, or Android.
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected.
  • Any error message you saw, if applicable.
  • Whether the issue relates to sign-in, subscription, story generation, audio, saved stories, reminders, or account deletion.
